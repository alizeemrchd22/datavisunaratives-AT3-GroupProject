# AI Provenance Matrix

This document records the use of AI tools during the development of the Data Narrative Studio project.

| Artefact / Task | Group Member | AI Tool | What Was Asked | What Came Back | Verification Performed | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chart D – New Organisations | Student C | ChatGPT | Assistance with developing and improving the Python implementation of the new organisations chart | Coding suggestions and guidance for calculating and visualising the number of new organisations entering the AI field each year | Checked the yearly organisation counts against the processed model dataset and verified the first recorded publication year used to identify new organisations | Reviewed, tested and modified before use |
| Chart E – Open-Weight Models | Student C | ChatGPT | Assistance with developing the open-weight percentage chart | Coding suggestions and guidance for calculating and displaying the yearly percentage of open-weight models | Recalculated yearly percentages using models classified as Yes and No and compared the results with the processed dataset | Reviewed, tested and modified before use |

## Verification Approach

For Chart D, the yearly organisation counts were checked against the processed dataset to confirm that organisations were counted according to their first recorded publication year.

For Chart E, the yearly open-weight percentages were checked against the Yes and No classifications in the processed dataset. Unknown classifications were excluded from the percentage calculation.

AI-generated suggestions were reviewed and tested before being incorporated into the final charts. The group member remained responsible for the final implementation and results.
